Frequently asked questions

How much does Photoshop cost?

Regularly at £21.98/mo Alternatively at £9.98/moincl. VAT for the annual billed monthly plan. Learn more about purchasing options on the Creative Cloud plans page.

Is there a free version of Photoshop?

Yes, Adobe Photoshop on mobile is a free mobile version of Adobe Photoshop with premium features that are available when you purchase a subscription. Add and remove objects, make targeted adjustments, collage and blend images and more — all on your phone. To download the app, visit the app store on your device. You can start a free trial of Adobe Photoshop to try the full desktop app.

Is there a free online version of Photoshop?

There isn’t a free version of Adobe Photoshop on the web, however you can try out some of the most popular tools in Adobe Photoshop with the help of guided tutorials without creating an Adobe account. To upload and edit your own photos, you’ll have to sign up for a Adobe Photoshop plan or start a free trial.

Is there student pricing for Photoshop?

Yes, students and teachers save over 75% on the Creative Cloud Pro plan. Find out more here.

Are there generative AI features for Photoshop?

Generative AI features like Generative Fill and Generative Expand are natively integrated into Adobe Photoshop, combining the power of AI with the precision of Adobe Photoshop, so you can create more with complete control. Add, expand and remove content in images using features powered by Adobe Firefly and then adjust them using the AI photo editing tools in Adobe Photoshop.
