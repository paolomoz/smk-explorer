    The Creative's Guide to Surviving (and Loving) Rejection

    Four years ago, a single rejection sent graphic designer Liz Mosley on a side quest she never could have predicted — one that started with a DM to Chris Do, CEO and Founder of The Futur — and ended up reshaping her entire career. That rejection sparked a challenge: To collect 100 of them. What she discovered changed everything. In this session, Liz and Chris unpack why your fear of rejection is the real thing holding you back, the stories you're telling yourself about what "no" means, and why collecting rejections might be the best career move you’re not making. Together they'll share: - Why a "no" doesn't mean what you think it does - How to turn rejection into momentum - What it actually means if you're not getting rejected - How to reframe fear as a signal — not a stop sign
